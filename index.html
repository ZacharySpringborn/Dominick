The three price reductions this week are consistent with the broader market signal: inventory is up 8.1% year-over-year and months of supply has grown to 3.1. Sellers who priced aggressively in the spring are starting to blink. That creates real opportunity for a buyer who is ready to move — you can negotiate from a position that did not exist six months ago.

The 14-day sale of CR 48½ in Milliken is a useful counterpoint: well-priced homes with acreage at the low end of this range are still getting absorbed quickly. The market is not broken — it is sorting itself. Overpriced inventory sits. Correctly priced inventory moves.

Cutter Dr’s BOM status reflects the same dynamic from the seller’s side: a deal fell apart, likely over price or inspection. A buyer who comes in clean with a strong pre-approval and reasonable asks has real leverage there.

Area Guide — Northern Colorado

Fort Collins
Home to CSU, Old Town, and a nationally recognized trail system. Inventory is growing, median SFR price at $665K. Half-acre lots at your price range are increasingly rare within city limits, but 4829 E CR 54 (2.3 ac, $739K) and 622 Boulder Cir are exceptions worth a look.
Loveland
Strong arts scene, I-25 access, and real acreage at prices just south of Fort Collins. Eagle Crest (503 Eagle Crest Ct) is an established custom-home neighborhood. 4801 Norwood Ave at 4.64 acres is the most acreage in this entire report for under $800K.
Berthoud
Small-town feel with equestrian properties and Forbes Ranch Estates custom builds. 722 Sophie St (now $950K after $49K cut) and 2809 Lake Hollow Rd are both worth a walk-through. Trade-off: slower resale liquidity than Fort Collins.
Severance & Windsor
Growing northeast corridors with newer builds. Cutter Dr (BOM, 2021 construction, 4,412 sqft) is the strongest value story in Severance right now. Prairie Song new construction (Windsor) offers 1 story/ranch layouts on just over an acre at the top of your budget.
Bellvue
801 Hernia Hill Trl: 11+ acres, 2013 build, $840K. True rural Colorado — Poudre Canyon corridor, privacy, dark skies, direct mountain access. Trade-off: limited cell coverage in some areas, winter road access, longer emergency response times.
Evans, Pierce & Johnstown
Largest sq footage per dollar in this search. Evans, Pierce, and Johnstown deliver square footage and acreage at the low end of your range. Best suited if raw space and land are your top priorities over proximity to Fort Collins services.
