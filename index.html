October shows a market with more choices and a slower pace. Single-family inventory in Fort Collins rose to 514 homes (+18.4%) and months of supply reached 3.3, up from 2.7 a year earlier. Pending sales fell 18.3%, closed sales fell 30.5%, and homes that sold took 73 days on average.

Price signals are mixed. The October median sales price was $675,000 (+9.8%), but that came from a small month of 116 closings. The year-to-date median is $630,000 (+0.8%), which is the steadier read. Sellers received 98.7% of list price in October.

The recent sales in your search fit that picture. The four homes that sold closed between 1% and 6% under their last asking prices. In a market with more inventory and fewer sales, sellers are willing to negotiate, and the buyers who are ready with an offer on a well-priced home benefit most.
